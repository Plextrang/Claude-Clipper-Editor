(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Each track page on the Free Music Archive (FMA) says so in the form
"<Title> by <Artist> is licensed under a CC0 1.0 Universal License" and links to the CC0 deed.
Licences checked on 2026-10-28. Attribution is not required, but crediting the artists is a nice thing to do.

Processing: each file was transcoded from the FMA source MP3 (320 kbps, or 128 kbps for Winter Rain) to MP3 at 160 kbps, 44.1 kHz stereo. It was loudness-normalised with two-pass ffmpeg `loudnorm` (target -16 LUFS, true peak -1.5 dBTP) and its metadata was stripped.
"Sub-150 Hz" is the share of spectral power below 150 Hz. No kept track goes above 0.45, so energy sits mostly in the mids and highs, which suits phone speakers.
FMA does not state a BPM for these tracks. The BPM values are estimated from onset autocorrelation and may be off by half or double.
